# Put a price beside its object

On a phone paired over HTTPS, select objects in the camera first. Each selection reserves its own job immediately. On a browser offering immersive AR, **공간 보기** starts one XR session for up to four textured selections, even before their prices are ready. Allow camera and spatial access, then point back at the products. You do not need to tap the floor. A price arriving during XR replaces the pending label. Leaving XR returns to the camera with the same investigations. In product details, **상품 옆에 자동 3D 표시** shows one selection.

| Runtime | What establishes a location | What happens on loss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal phone or Mac camera | Image-space contour and feature tracking; no metric depth | Hide after a short grace period. Job and results remain accessible in 조사 목록. |
| Immersive WebXR, automatic | Compare a sparse RGB sample from inside the SAM contour against view-aligned XR camera pixels. Two consistent observations plus a coherent group of measured depths locate the object in local world coordinates. | Hide the card when appearance, depth or pose is missing or ambiguous. Confirm the target again before reusing its price. |
| ARKit / ARCore companion | The user opens one product from its web details. The app confirms the sparse signature against its own camera and estimates metric position from measured depth or distributed tracked features. | Moving the target updates the spatial placement. Unconfirmed matches stay hidden. See [iOS](../native/ios/README.md) and [Android](../native/android/README.md). |
| Manual surface placement | Choose **표면 직접 배치** in the web result, or the corresponding native mode; tap a detected shelf or floor. | The card stays at that surface even if the product moves. This mode never supplies an automatic object's depth. |

Automatic WebXR needs a secure context, user gesture, immersive AR, WebGL, camera-access and CPU depth-sensing. A browser may support immersive AR without exposing those last two capabilities. When permission or support is missing there is no synthetic distance. The ordinary camera page remains available. On iPhone use the installed ARKit companion when Safari does not provide the required session. These companions do not extend Apple's or Google's stock Camera app.

The web renderer uses the eye's asymmetric projection, pose and axial depth at masked coordinates. It draws a translucent 22 cm billboard beside the observed silhouette, with viewing perspective for each eye. Where the browser supports anchors it uses XRAnchor; otherwise a measured position is held in the current local reference space. A reset invalidates the old world position. When looking away, there is no fixed screen label pointing offscreen. No stored room map, general rotation-invariant instance recognition or furniture occlusion mesh is claimed. Identical copies, flat colors, glare, severe rotation, or an unseen side may prevent a lock. Sparse RGB samples are local visual matching data, not embeddings or a product search key. A native handoff includes them but never the full photograph; do not log the URI.

**Reproduce:** run `npm test` and `npm run smoke:world-anchor`. The latter uses a real Chromium WebGL texture with controlled camera, pose and depth input. It exercises motion, loss/return, asynchronous prices, independent anchors, session cleanup and the separate stationary path. To measure accuracy and latency on a physical phone, follow [field gate #31](https://github.com/NomaDamas/HowMuch/issues/31); simulated poses and unsigned builds do not establish these results.
